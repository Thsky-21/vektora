HOW TO REGISTER:
 Students: You cannot register directly. Your college’s SPOC

02WHO CAN BE SPOC:
The SPOC (Single Point of Contact) for Smart India Hackathon 2026
can be any senior member of the college faculty, such as Lecturer
Assistant Professor, Associate Professor, Professor, Head of
Department (HOD), Dean, Director, Principal or another designated
official.
The SPOC is typically responsible for coordinating the internal hackathon,
managing student registrations, and ensuring that all guidelines are followed for
the institution's participation in the event.

--- p14
TEAM
NOMINATION
PROCESS
It is mandatory that the College SPOC enters the details for a
nominated team in the prescribed format only (Annexure A, also can
be downloaded from the SPOC dashboard). Team details once entered
cannot be altered. College SPOC will be responsible to ensure that all
the data entered by him/ her is accurate
The letter must be issued on college letterhead only. It must clearly
state name of the team and all 6 team members and upto 2
Faculty/Industry Mentor and must be duly signed by college principal/
dean/ institute director and bear the college seal. This letter will be
required during the grand finale, if the team is selected for grand
finale
The team name should be unique and must not contain the name of
your institute in any form
A max of 50 teams (45 Shortlisted + 05 Waitlisted) per college and in
case of university 100 Team can be nominated (including Problem
statement based/Student innovation category)
There is no bar for the Software and hardware categories. You can
make the combination as per your convenience but the total number of
the teams should not breach 50 team’s criteria for institute and 100 for
university.

IDEA SUBMISSION PROCESS 10
Idea submission counter will start from Aug 2026 and only 500 ideas will be submitted for a
particular PS. Once the count number got all 500 ideas, the particular PS will get freeze and
no idea submission will be allowed for the respective PS
Count number with the ideas submitted numbers and Ideas left numbers will be displayed
in the public domain just opposite to the PS and can be viewed by anyone on sih.gov.in
One team can submit Ideas against maximum of 2 Problem Statement only
Nominated Team’s team leader will get the Login credentials after SPOC upload team
details in the portal to complete the idea submission process
The last date for team nomination and idea submission by College SPOC and Team leader
on SIH portal is till 30th Sept 2026 only. No request will be entertained after the deadline
SPOC registration will start from July 2026 & the last date for team nomination and
idea submission by College SPOC on SIH portal is till 30  Sep 2026.th

MODE OF SIH GRAND FINALE
Post Idea submission process, the ideas will be evaluated by experts.
Evaluation criteria will include novelty of the idea, complexity, clarity and
details in the prescribed format, feasibility, practicability, sustainability, scale
of impact, user experience and potential for future work progression.
13
IDEA SELECTION CRITERIA
SIH 2026 Grand Finale will be held offline at various nodal centers across pan
India and teams have to travelled to their respective nodal centers.The Grand
Finale of Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026 is proposed to be organized in
December 2026

--- p21
SIH 2026
 MENTORS ELIGIBILITY 
14
For the SIH Grand Finale, after the teams are shortlisted, each team will
have the option to select up to 2 mentors from industry or academia. A
mentor is expected to possess relevant experience( 5 years)  in industry
and/or academic domains to qualify.
Each team, consisting of 6 student members, may include up to 2
mentors in addition to the core student team. However, the
participation of mentors along with the team is optional and entirely at
the discretion of the team.

GENERAL INFORMATION REGARDING THE
GRAND FINALE
16
If a team is selected for the final Hackathon, members
will need to travel to the assigned nodal center which
can be anywhere in India
No person—whether a faculty member, family member,
friend, or anyone else—apart from the officially
registered student team members and mentors will be
allowed to participate in or stay at the hackathon venue.
Additionally, no reimbursement claims from any such
unregistered individuals will be entertained.
Shortlisted teams along with mentor traveling to the
grand finale are eligible for The maximum ceiling of
travel reimbursement is Rs 3,000/- per person (subject
to actuals) for round trip including local mileage, if any
based on production the actual bills irrespective of
any mode of travel
The nodal centers will be arranging for
accommodation of teams during the grand finale
Each college/institute will issue a stamped photo ID to
each member of the teams selected for the finale
College photo ID and Consent letter copy is
mandatory for participating in the finale

PROBLEM
STATEMENTS
AND PRIZES 
18
There would be ONE single winning team for every
problem statement posted on the portal. The prize
money will be given by the collaborating ministry/
industry ONLY IF that organization likes the idea of
the winning team. However, it is NOT mandatory to
announce only one winning team against each
problem statement. 
SIH2026 brings you problem
statements from leading public and
private organizations, union
ministries, state governments and
NGOs in India
Amount of prize money to be
awarded to each winning team is
Rs.1,50,000/- per problem statement
If there is a tie between two or more teams, the final
decision of the prize money distribution will be
taken by the problem statement creator only and
once the decision is made, it won’t be changed
further
Teams are encouraged to think out-of-the-box and
provide creative solutions to themes under ‘Students
Innovation’ category.
